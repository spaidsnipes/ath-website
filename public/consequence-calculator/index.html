How these estimates are calculated
  • Cost per contact = cost per lead ÷ contact rate.
  • Cost per appointment = cost per lead ÷ (contact rate × booking rate).
  • Cost per customer = cost per lead ÷ (contact rate × booking rate × show rate × close rate).
  • Each leak line is the revenue that stage's drop-off would have produced if it had continued at your own later-stage rates, one stage at a time. The monthly leak is the sum of the three. Your close rate is left as you entered it.
  • These are estimates from the numbers you typed, not a forecast and not a promise of what ATH will recover. Real results depend on your leads, team and market.
