Every ATH AI says it is an AI. A person at ATH sets it up, and your customers can always reach a person.

What it does

  • Answers when you can't. During jobs, lunch, busy hours and after hours.
  • Books into what you already use. Your calendar or booking tool stays the same.
  • Takes a clear message. Name, number, what they need and how urgent, sent to you by text.
  • Texts back missed callers from your business number so the conversation keeps going.
  • Hands off to a person. Anything it can't handle goes to you or your team, with the notes.

What it won't do

  • Pretend to be a person. It says it is an AI.
  • Give medical, legal or financial advice.
  • Record a call without consent, or text people who haven't agreed to it. Opt-outs work on every flow.
