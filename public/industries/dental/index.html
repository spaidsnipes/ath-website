The real problem

Missed calls

Calls during procedures, lunch and after hours go to voicemail, and many callers never leave one.

Slow follow-up

Web and ad inquiries wait until someone has a free minute. By then the patient has booked somewhere else.

No easy booking

Patients who want to book at 9 PM have no way to start except calling back tomorrow.

Lapsed recall

Patients overdue for cleanings sit in your system with no reminder that reaches them.

Reviews by chance

Reviews get asked for when someone remembers, so your profile doesn't reflect how many patients you serve.

What ATH does

  • AI receptionist. Answers when the desk can't, handles scheduling and general questions (hours, location, insurance accepted, new-patient steps), and books or takes a message. It does not give clinical advice.
  • Missed-call text-back. A caller who hangs up gets an automatic text back from your practice number, so the conversation keeps going.
  • Speed-to-lead. Form and ad inquiries get an immediate reply and a handoff to your team.
  • Review requests, no gating. Every patient gets the same review request after a visit. We never screen out unhappy patients.
  • Recall reactivation. Overdue patients you choose get a friendly reminder to book.
  • Website. A mobile site with one clear call and booking path.

Patient privacy. ATH does not claim HIPAA compliance on this page. Before any dental install we map exactly what patient information each system would see, keep clinical details out of automated messages, and confirm with you whether a Business Associate Agreement is needed with ATH or any vendor involved. If it can't be done the right way for your practice, we say so in the scope.
