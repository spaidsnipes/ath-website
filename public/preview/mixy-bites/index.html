Something savory,
something sweet.

From the café’s own DoorDash menu (October 2026). Full menu and current prices are on DoorDash.

Rice combo meals

Bowl (entrée + rice) or platter (entrée + rice + lumpiang shanghai + pancit bihon).

  • Adobo Rice ComboPork belly and/or chicken in soy, vinegar and garlic
  • Beef Tapa Rice ComboMarinated, garlicky, savory-sweet beef
  • Pork Belly Sisig Rice ComboSpicy or non-spicy
  • Sinigang Rice ComboSour tamarind soup
  • Pinakbet Rice Combo
  • Pork Dinuguan Rice Combo
  • Embutido Rice Combo

Lumpia & pancit

Crispy golden spring rolls and Filipino noodles.

  • Pork Lumpiang Shanghai6 pieces or a dozen
  • Lumpia & PancitPancit bihon with pork lumpiang shanghai
  • Palabok & LumpiaRice noodles in savory orange sauce
  • Pancit Palabok A La Carte
  • Pancit Bihon A La Carte

Filipino sweet treats

Cold, creamy and colorful.

  • Halo-haloShaved ice, sweet beans, fruits, jellies and leche flan
  • Sweet Buko Pandan SaladCoconut and pandan jelly in sweet milk
  • Ice Scramble
  • Cheesy Sweet Corn
  • Samalamig
  • Buko Juice PalamigYoung coconut juice with coconut strips

Milk tea & drinks

Milk teas, frappes, iced coffee and dirty sodas.

  • Brown Sugar Milk Tea
  • Ube Macapuno Milk Tea or Frappe
  • Buko Pandan Milk Tea or Frappe
  • Taro, Thai, Matcha & more milk teas
  • Dulce de Tiramisu Frappe
  • Iced coffee & dirty soda

From the bakery case

Baked treats to go with your drink.

  • CroffleCroissant-waffle with a choice of drizzle
  • Stuffed cookies
  • Cake pops
